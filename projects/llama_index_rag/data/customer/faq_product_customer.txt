Frequently Asked Questions: Product Usage

How do I reset my password?
Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password." You'll receive a reset link by email within a few minutes.

Is there a mobile app?
Yes, our app is available on both iOS and Android. Search for our product name in the App Store or Google Play.

How do I upgrade my plan?
Visit Account Settings > Plan & Billing, then select "Upgrade" next to the plan you'd like to switch to. Changes take effect immediately.

Can I use the product offline?
Some features are available offline, but syncing requires an internet connection. Changes made offline will sync automatically once you're back online.

Who do I contact for technical issues?
You can reach our support team via the in-app chat or the Help Center contact form. For urgent issues, please include your account email and a description of the problem.
