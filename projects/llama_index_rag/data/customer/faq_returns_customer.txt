Frequently Asked Questions: Returns & Refunds

How do I request a refund?
You can request a refund within 30 days of purchase by visiting your Order History and clicking "Request Refund" next to the relevant order.

How long does a refund take?
Most refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after approval. You'll receive an email confirmation once your refund has been issued.

What items are eligible for a refund?
Most items are eligible within 30 days if unused and in original packaging. Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable.

Can I cancel a subscription and get a refund?
Yes, you can cancel anytime from Account Settings > Subscriptions. If you cancel mid-cycle, you may be eligible for a prorated refund depending on your plan.

What if my refund is delayed?
If it has been more than 10 business days, please contact our support team through the Help Center, and we'll look into it for you.
