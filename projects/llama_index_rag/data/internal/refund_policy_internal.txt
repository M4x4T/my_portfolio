INTERNAL POLICY: Refund Processing Procedure

This document is for internal staff only. Do not share with customers.

Refund Approval Thresholds:
- Refunds under $50: any support agent can approve directly in the system.
- Refunds $50-$500: requires team lead approval via the internal ticketing system.
- Refunds over $500: requires manager approval and finance sign-off within 2 business days.

Internal Processing Steps:
1. Verify the order in the internal admin panel (Orders > Search by Order ID).
2. Check the refund reason code against the internal reason-code list (see Appendix A in the wiki).
3. If the customer is flagged as "high-risk" in the CRM, escalate to the fraud review team before processing.
4. Refunds are processed through the internal payment gateway dashboard, not the customer-facing site.
5. Log the refund in the internal tracking sheet with agent ID and timestamp.

Special Cases:
- Subscription cancellations mid-cycle: prorate based on internal formula (days_used / days_in_cycle).
- Refunds tied to a known bug (see internal bug tracker tag "refund-impact"): auto-approve regardless of amount, tag ticket "bug-refund".

Escalation Contacts:
- Finance sign-off: internal finance Slack channel #finance-refunds
- Fraud review: fraud-team@internal (internal email alias, not public)
