INTERNAL PROCEDURE: New Employee Onboarding (Support Team)

Week 1:
- Set up access to internal admin panel, CRM, and ticketing system.
- Shadow two senior support agents on live chat sessions.
- Complete internal product training modules 1-3 (found on internal LMS).

Week 2:
- Begin handling tier-1 tickets under supervision.
- Review internal escalation matrix: which issue types go to Tier 2, Engineering, or Billing.

Internal Tools Access Required:
- Admin panel (read-only initially, write access after 30 days)
- Internal knowledge base (Confluence space "Support-Internal")
- CRM system with customer history and internal risk flags

Note: New agents should NOT have access to the fraud review queue or refund approval above $50 until 60-day review is complete.
